# Launch Summary

Product: Deposit Defender Desk

Status: LOCAL_COMPLETE_NOT_PUBLISHED

## Positioning

Deposit Defender Desk is a local-first rental deposit evidence desk that turns move-in/move-out records and landlord deductions into an evidence gap map, deadline check, and dispute packet outline.
